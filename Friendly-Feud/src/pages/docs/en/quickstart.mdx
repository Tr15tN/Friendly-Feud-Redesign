# Host quickstart

Everything you need to run your first game, in about five minutes.
For the full rules and every control, read the [Game Guide](/docs/en/guide).

> **Short version:** Host a game → load questions → share the room code → open the board on a TV →
> Start Round 1 → reveal answers, add strikes, bank the points → Fast Money.

## Before you start

You need:

- **One device for you** (laptop, tablet or phone). This is the control panel.
- **A screen for the players** (TV, projector or a second laptop) to show the board. Optional, but it makes the game.
- **Phones for the players** so they can buzz. Optional: you can also use one shared buzzer.

## 1. Create the room

On the home page press **Host**. You get a 4-letter **room code** at the top left. Anyone can host their own game,
and several games can run at the same time. A game is closed after an hour without activity.

The first time, a short tour walks you through the screen. You can replay it any time from the **?** menu in the top bar.

## 2. Load the questions

In **Choose a game** pick a built-in question set, or upload your own `.json` or `.csv` file. The list follows the
language you picked in **Language & look**.

Want your own questions? Press **Create New Game**, fill in rounds and Fast Money, and load the file it gives you.

Below that you can set team names, a title, a logo and the board theme.

## 3. Put the board on the big screen

Press **Open Game Window**. A new tab opens with the board. Move it to the TV or projector.
You keep playing from the control panel.

On a separate device, open the site, press **Play** with any name and the room code, then choose **Open Game Window**
instead of a team.

## 4. Let players join

Players open the site on their phone, type the **room code** and a name, pick a team and press **Play**.
They appear in the **Lobby**, in their team. When everyone is in, press **Start Round 1**.

## 5. Run a round

1. Players **buzz**. The first team is marked as in control.
2. Click an answer to reveal it, or press the keys **1-8**. Click again to hide it.
3. Wrong answer? Press **Strike** for that team. At three strikes the other team gets a chance to steal.
4. Press **Bank** for the team that wins the round (the button shows the points, for example **Bank 66**). The points are added and the round locks.
5. Press **Next Round** and repeat.

Made a mistake? **Undo** (or **Ctrl/Cmd + Z**) steps back through your scoring actions.

## 6. Fast Money

After the last round use **Fast Money** in the top bar. Type each answer, pick the matching board answer,
press **Reveal Answer** and **Award points**. **Start Timer** runs the countdown on the board.

## Using a real buzzer

A USB buzzer that sends a key press (for example **Enter**) works great.

1. On the host console, press **Host Buzzers** in the top bar. It opens in a new tab: drag that tab to the laptop next to the buzzer (or open the site there, press **Play** with any name and the room code, then choose **Host Buzzers**).
2. Enter the **host password** from **Settings** on your control panel.
3. Press **Buzzer keys** to bind the key your buzzer sends. You can bind several keys.
4. When someone presses the buzzer you get a **Who was first?** window. Pick the team, or press **1** / **2**.

## Handy to know

- **Settings** (gear icon, or below the lobby) hold teams, logo, theme, sounds and the host password.
- **Players** shows who has buzzed and lets you clear buzzers, blindfold or remove players.
- **Flow** jumps to another round or back to the title screen.
- Lost connection? Reopen the site. Your browser remembers the room and puts you back.
- Several people can watch the board. Only you, the host, can change the game.
