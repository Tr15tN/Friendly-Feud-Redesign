# Friendly Feud — Game Guide

A complete guide to running and playing Friendly Feud. This describes exactly what this build
does. Where the game leaves a decision to you, it is marked as a **House rule** suggestion.

> **TL;DR for the host:** Host a room → open the game window on the TV → players join on their
> phones with the room code → Start Round 1 → reveal answers, mark strikes, bank the pot →
> repeat → Fast Money → read the final scores.

## 1. Quick start for the host

1. **Create a room** — click **Host**. You get a 4‑letter **room code** and a host password
   that travels automatically with your controls (you never type it).
2. **Load a game** in **Titles, teams & settings** — a built‑in game or your own `.json`/`.csv`.
   Set team names, title, logo, and theme here too.
3. **Open the game window on the TV** — **Open Game Window** opens the board (`/game`) in a new
   tab; put it on the TV/projector.
4. **Players join on phones** — enter the **room code**, type a name, tap **Join**, pick a
   team, press **Play** to arm the buzzer.
5. **Spectators / extra screens** — instead of a team, choose **Open Game Window** (show the
   board elsewhere) or **Host Buzzers** (a shared two‑button buzzer).
6. **Start the game** — press **Start Round 1**.

## 2. The host console at a glance

The console has three phases shown in the phase bar: **Lobby → Rounds N/N → Fast Money**.
There is always one obvious primary (blue) button for the current phase; other controls are
secondary, and a one‑line hint says what to do next.

## 3. Main rounds

### 3.1 The face‑off (buzzers)

Buzzers clear when a round starts. Players buzz from their phones (or via the shared **Host
Buzzers** screen). The board shows **who buzzed first**, and the console marks that team
“buzzed first / in control”.

The “in control” marker is **informational** — the game does not enforce turn order or lock
anyone out. **House rule:** play it like classic Feud — the fastest players face off on the
top answer; the higher answer wins control and chooses to play or pass.

### 3.2 Revealing answers

Click an answer (or press keys **1–8**) to reveal it on the board; click/press again to hide
it. Revealed answers add to the **round pot**; cards show `points × multiplier`. The question
text is **hidden on the TV by default** — toggle it with **Show / Hide questions**.

### 3.3 Strikes, 3 strikes, and the steal

Give a wrong‑answer **strike** with **✕ Team**. Each team has three strike slots (capped at 3).

- **Buzz‑in X (no strike)** flashes the big ✕ on the board without adding a strike; the team
  **✕** buttons add a real strike.
- Fix with **− strike (Team)** or **Reset Mistakes**.
- At **3 strikes** a yellow hint says the other team can steal. The app only **hints** — you
  decide and press the award button. **House rule:** on a steal the other team gives one
  answer; if it’s on the board they steal the whole pot, otherwise control keeps it.

### 3.4 How the round pot is calculated

> pot = (sum of the points of every revealed answer) × round multiplier

The **multiplier** gives Double (×2) and Triple (×3) rounds — set per round in the game file
or changed live with the ×1/×2/×3 buttons or the × number box. A blank/invalid multiplier is
treated as ×1. Hiding a revealed answer removes its value again.

### 3.5 Banking / awarding the pot

Press **Award N to [team]** to give the pot to a team. That adds it to their score and **locks
the round**: the award buttons and multiplier are disabled, and the board shows “Banked by
[team]”.

### 3.6 Answers revealed after banking (not scored)

After an award the pot is locked to what was banked. Revealing leftover answers marks them
**(not scored)** and dimmed on the TV; they do not change any score.

### 3.7 Fixing mistakes: Undo and Manual adjust

**Undo** (↶ Undo, or **Ctrl/Cmd+Z**) steps back through scoring actions (up to 50) without
touching players or settings. **Manual adjust** adds/subtracts a fixed number of points to a
team (never below 0).

### 3.8 Worked scoring example

Answers: Black Hood & Cape 43, Sickle 20, Skeleton 13, Mask 11, Makeup 8. Round set to **×2**.

1. Reveal Black Hood & Cape (43) → pot = 43 × 2 = **86**.
2. Reveal Skeleton (13) → pot = (43 + 13) × 2 = **112**.
3. Alpha gets a 3rd strike; Bravo steals with a correct answer.
4. **Award 112 to Bravo** → Bravo **+112**, round locks.
5. Reveal Makeup (8) to show the board → **(not scored)**; pot stays **112**, no score change.

## 4. Fast Money (the final round)

Press **Fast Money**. Two contestants play in turn against the same questions; the goal is a
combined score that reaches the **win target**.

### 4.1 How it’s played

Per question row: type the contestant’s **Answer**, pick the matching board answer (and points)
from the **Award points** dropdown (or leave **(No Answer) 0**), press **Reveal Answer**, then
**Award points**. A **(No Answer)** award plays the try‑again sound and shows **✗ 0** — it does
not flash a strike.

Press **Start Final Round 2** for the second contestant. The second half hides the first
contestant’s answers until you **Reveal First Round Answers**, and shows the partner’s answer so
you can judge duplicates.

### 4.2 Timers

Each half has its own countdown (`final_round_timers`, default 20s and 25s). Use **Start /
Stop / Reset Timer**. The board’s circular timer counts down and notifies the console at zero.

### 4.3 Duplicate answers

In the second half, press **Already Answered** on a question if the contestant repeats the
first contestant’s answer; it plays the duplicate buzzer. Judging duplicates is the host’s call.

### 4.4 The total and win target

**Total** = points from both halves. **Target** defaults to **200** and is set per game in
**Titles, teams & settings → Fast money target**. Reaching or exceeding the target shows **WIN**
with confetti. **House rule:** classic Fast Money uses 5 questions and a target of 200.

## 5. Winning and the end of the game

The app does **not** declare a main‑game winner — it keeps the team totals and **you read the
scores**. **House rule:** higher score after your agreed number of rounds wins, or the leader
plays Fast Money. The only automatic “win” is the Fast Money target celebration. To wrap up,
show the **Title Card** again or press **Quit**.

## 6. Keyboard shortcuts

Host console (ignored while typing, on the title screen, and during Fast Money):

- **1–8** — reveal / hide the matching answer in the current round.
- **Ctrl/Cmd + Z** — undo the last scoring action.

Shared Host Buzzers screen:

- **j** — buzz for Team 1 (Alpha). **k** — buzz for Team 2 (Bravo). (Wire two physical
  keyboard buttons as buzzers.)
- Any **bound buzzer key** (default **Enter**; change it with **Buzzer keys**) — a shared
  buzzer was pressed. The host gets **Who was first?** and picks the team (**1** / **2** also work).

## 7. Host console reference

**Top bar:** Room code · Open Game Window · Help menu (tour, quickstart, this guide) · Quit (host only).

**Flow controls:** Phase bar · Title Card (shows title; starts fresh, double‑click to confirm
mid‑game) · Start Round 1 · Round selector · Next Round → (clears strikes, fresh pot, clears
buzzers) · Show / Hide questions · Final Round.

**Lobby:** big join code (and “Join at [URL]” if set), per‑team roster, Title Music.

**Score bar:** each team’s name, score, three strike pips, banked / buzzed‑first tag; centre
shows round, Pot (or “Banked by [team]”), ×1/×2/×3 and × box, and ↶ Undo.

**Answer grid:** one button per answer (keys 1–8); base → multiplied value, **(not scored)**
tag after an award, last‑action undo pill.

**Strikes & Bank the pot:** ✕ Team (add) · − strike (Team) · Reset Mistakes · Buzz‑in X (no
strike) · steal hint at 3 strikes · Award N to [team] · Manual adjust.

**Fast Money controls:** Start Final Round 2 / Back To Final Round 1 · Reveal / Hide First
Round Answers · Start / Stop / Reset Timer · per question: Answer, Award‑points dropdown,
Reveal, Award, status, and (round 2) the partner’s answer with Already Answered · Round one /
two / Total and Target.

**Players & buzzers:** Buzzer Order with Clear Buzzers; Players list with per‑player blindfold
and remove.

## 8. Creating your own games

Use the built‑in creator at **/new** (or **Create New Game**), or hand‑write `.json` / import
`.csv`.

**/new creator:** add rounds (question, multiplier 1/2/3, Answer + points pairs); add Fast
Money questions (Answer + points) and the two timer values; **Save** downloads a `.json` to
load back via **Load Game**.

**JSON essentials:**

```json
{
  "settings": { "theme": "default", "final_round_win_threshold": 200 },
  "rounds": [
    {
      "question": "Name something you'd need to look like the Grim Reaper.",
      "multiply": 1,
      "answers": [
        { "ans": "Black Hood & Cape", "pnt": 43 },
        { "ans": "Sickle", "pnt": 20 }
      ]
    }
  ],
  "final_round": [
    {
      "question": "Name a part of the body beginning with N.",
      "answers": [
        ["Nose", 52],
        ["Neck", 34]
      ]
    }
  ],
  "final_round_timers": [20, 25]
}
```

Main‑round answers are `{ "ans", "pnt" }`; Fast Money answers are `["text", points]` pairs.
`multiply` is the Double/Triple multiplier; `final_round_win_threshold` is optional (default
200).

**CSV:** one round per line — `Question, Answer, Points, Answer, Points, ...`. On import you
choose how many rows are rounds vs Fast Money.

## 9. Player guide (short)

Join with the room code and a name, pick a team, press **Play**. On a face‑off tap **BUZZ**
(buzzers reset each round). You see your team, score, and strikes; a host can “blindfold” your
view. Not playing? Choose **Open Game Window** or **Host Buzzers** instead of a team.

## 10. Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Reconnecting:** your browser remembers your session; reopening usually puts you back in. A
“room not found” means the room was closed or timed out — rejoin with a fresh code.

**Host the board on a separate device:** host on your controller device, then on the TV device
**Join** and press **Open Game Window** instead of picking a team. For a shared physical
buzzer, pick **Host Buzzers**, enter the host password shown on the admin page; that screen
responds to **j** and **k**, and to any key you bind under **Buzzer keys**.

**Security note:** only the **host** (the browser that created the room, carrying the host
password) can control a room — reveal, score, change settings, or quit. Players, spectators,
and people in other rooms cannot, even if they know the room code. Keep the host tab/session to
yourself.

**The game window isn’t updating:** reload the game‑window tab (it rejoins from your saved
session) and make sure you’re acting in the host tab, not a spectator/player tab.
